# Code of conduct

SeyLock follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- be respectful;
- assume good intent;
- criticise ideas, not people;
- no harassment of any kind.

Many people who use or discuss this library live under censorship and
surveillance. Never ask anyone to reveal where they are, which networks they
use, or who they are, and never repeat such details if someone shares them.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Yurii Arkhanhelskyi, at
pilot979797@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential.
